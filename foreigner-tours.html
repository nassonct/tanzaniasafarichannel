International Preparation & Peace of Mind

A carefree expedition starts with seamless arrival logistics. Review our mandatory travel checklist designed for international arrivals.

01 / VISA & BORDER badge

Visa & Entry Formalities

Online Tanzania eVisa processing is recommended 30 days prior. Visas-on-arrival remain available at Kilimanjaro (JRO) and Dar es Salaam (DAR) for eligible nationalities. Your passport must hold 6 months validity beyond your departure date and 3 blank stamped pages.

Electronic Entry Support Provided
02 / HEALTH health_and_safety

Health & Vaccinations

Yellow Fever vaccination certificates are mandatory if transiting through endemic nations. We advise consulting your travel clinic for malaria prophylactics (Malarone/Doxycycline). All our guests are automatically enrolled in Flying Doctors (AMREF) emergency wilderness evacuation coverage.

AMREF Medevac Included
03 / TRANSIT SPECS luggage

Luggage & Packing Advisory

Light domestic bush aircraft maintain a strict 15 kg (33 lbs) baggage limit per passenger inside soft-sided duffel bags (no rigid frames). Pack earth-toned neutral apparel (khaki, taupe, olive). Avoid dark navy and black attire which attracts tsetse flies in woodland river belts.

Complimentary Duffel Bag Provided
04 / FISCAL ADVICE payments

Currency & Payments

All listed prices are quoted in Tanzanian Shillings (TZS). US Dollars are also accepted; banks in East Africa may reject bills printed before 2009 or those with tears or stamps. Major Visa and Mastercards are supported at prime lodges, though a 3-5% bank processing fee is common. Tip allocation is customary in cash at safari end.

USD Bills Dated 2013+ Recommended
