Expedition Advisory

Frequently Asked Pre-Booking Questions

Essential insights regarding our bespoke curation process, season availability, and payment procedures.

01

How far in advance should I book my safari?

For peak Great Migration river crossings (July through October) and the festive Christmas/New Year period, we recommend securing tented lodges 8 to 12 months in advance due to strict conservation limits on luxury camp bed nights in the Mara & Lamai sectors.

02

Can I customize any package on this website?

Yes, 100% of our published itineraries are fully tailored. Whether adjusting night durations in Ngorongoro Crater, upgrading to private bush-plane charters, or integrating a bespoke Zanzibar spices and dhow sailing expedition, every plan is individually built.

03

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept international SWIFT wire transfers directly to our primary escrow accounts in Tanzania, as well as major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, and American Express) through our encrypted merchant gateway. All bookings are safeguarded under registered Tanzanian Tourism Licensing.
