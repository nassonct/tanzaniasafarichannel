Operational FAQ

Critical Field Protocols

Answers regarding communication dead-zones, vehicle repairs, and cross-border safari movements.

What happens if a vehicle breaks down in the Central Serengeti?

All vehicles operate in designated sectors with paired sister vehicles. Our Arusha fleet workshop coordinates rapid rescue units stationed year-round at Seronera and Ndutu airstrips. Backup vehicles are dispatched within 45 minutes to avoid itinerary interruption.

Is there cell signal or Wi-Fi while in the bush?

Most luxury tented camps maintain satellite Wi-Fi in mess tents. During game drives, 4G signals exist near major park gates and central kopjes (Airtel & Vodacom networks). Our guides carry backup satellite messengers and multi-channel radios for zero-coverage valleys.

How do we connect with our driver upon landing at Kilimanjaro Airport (JRO)?

Our uniformed airport host awaits you immediately outside international baggage claim at Desk 4, bearing a personalized brass plaque with your party name. They manage cold water refreshments, luggage handling, and direct private transfer to your Arusha lodge.
