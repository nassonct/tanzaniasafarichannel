Barefoot Luxury in Zanzibar & Mnemba

Transition effortlessly to the balmy Indian Ocean breeze. Kick off your boots and step onto powder-soft coral sands for total indulgence, quiet solitude, and sun-drenched coastal romance.

  • Private sunset sailing aboard a handcrafted wooden dhow with chilled vintage reserve champagne.
  • Pristine coral reef drift snorkeling & swimming alongside wild dolphins around private atolls.
  • Lantern-lit barefoot seafood feasts prepared with fragrant Swahili spices directly on the sand.
