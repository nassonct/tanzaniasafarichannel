Natural Wonders

The Great Migration

Witness one of the most spectacular events in the natural world. The Great Migration is not just a movement; it's a relentless, ancient quest for survival that defines the rhythm of the Serengeti.

From the calving season in the southern plains to the perilous river crossings in the north, millions of hooves thunder across the earth, followed closely by the park's formidable predators. It is a raw, unscripted drama of birth, struggle, and survival played out on a stage of infinite beauty.

  • Calving Season: January to March
  • River Crossings: July to September
